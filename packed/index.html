Packed

A trip bag checklist for iPhone and Apple Watch, so you stop forgetting things — and stop wondering where you put them.

Pack with proof

Take a photo of each item as you pack it. A trip isn't "done" until everything on the list has its photo.

"Where is my passport?"

Ask out loud and get an answer — your question is transcribed and matched against your own packed items, entirely on your iPhone.

On your wrist too

Check items off and glance at your trip from the Apple Watch app, kept in sync with your iPhone.

Repack in one tap

Coming home, going somewhere else — mark a leg as packed and start the next one without rebuilding the list from scratch.
