OneLocal Packed

Privacy Policy

For the Packed iPhone and Apple Watch app, published by OneLocal.

Packed has no account and no server of its own. Your trips, bags and items are yours — stored on your devices and, if you turn iCloud sync on, kept private between your own devices through your own iCloud account. This page explains exactly what the app touches and why.

What the app uses, and why

  • Your trips, bags and items. Stored locally in the app's own on-device database. If you enable sync in Settings, the same data is kept across your iPhone and Apple Watch privately through your own iCloud account — OneLocal has no access to it either way.
  • Camera. Takes a photo of each item as you pack it, as proof it went in. Photos stay on your iPhone.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Lets you ask things like "where is my passport?" out loud. What you say is transcribed on your iPhone and matched against your own stored items — it is never sent to a server.
  • On-device intelligence. Understanding your spoken question and finding the right item normally runs entirely on your iPhone. For some requests, iOS may route part of the processing through Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designed to be privacy-preserving and independently verifiable — see Apple's own Privacy Policy for how that works. OneLocal has no access to this either way.
  • Apple Watch. Mirrors your current trip and checklist to your Watch over Apple's on-device WatchConnectivity framework, staying strictly between your own paired devices.
  • Live Activity. Shown on your Lock Screen while Packed is actively listening for a spoken question. It displays no data beyond that.
  • Photo library (optional). You can attach an existing photo instead of taking a new one, through Apple's standard picker — the app only sees the photo you choose.

What OneLocal can see

Nothing. Packed has no backend, no analytics, and no crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. There is no way for us to see your trips, your recordings, or how you use the app.

Third parties

Packed does not use any third-party services, trackers or ad networks. iCloud sync and on-device intelligence are Apple's own first-party frameworks, governed by Apple's own privacy policy, not a third party OneLocal integrates separately.

Children's privacy

Packed is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Your choices

Everything lives in the app's own storage or your own iCloud account, so deleting a trip, turning off sync, or deleting the app removes that data. There is no separate OneLocal copy to request or delete.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does ever changes, this page will be updated to match before that change ships.
