OneLocal Packed

Privacy Policy

For the Packed iPhone and Apple Watch app, published by OneLocal. This policy is written to meet GDPR's Article 13/14 disclosure requirements.

Packed has no account and no server of its own. Your trips, bags and items are stored on your devices and, if you turn sync on, kept private between your own devices through your own iCloud account.

1.Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is OneLocal (Thomas Chézières), an individual developer based in France. For anything in this policy: support.packed@onelocal.dev. No separate Data Protection Officer is appointed — OneLocal does not carry out large-scale or systematic processing of the kind that requires one, and in practice processes none of your personal data on any server it operates (see below).

None of this is ever transmitted to OneLocal. There is no backend server, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting or advertising SDK in Packed. iCloud sync and the on-device assistant (which may occasionally use Apple's Private Cloud Compute for part of a request — see Apple's own Privacy Policy) are Apple's own first-party services; OneLocal has no access to that data either way.

3.Data Packed does not collect

No account, no email address, no advertising identifier, no location, no contacts, no precise usage analytics. Packed does not use cookies or any similar tracking technology, and does not perform any automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22).

4.International data transfers

OneLocal itself does not transfer any of your data internationally, since it operates no server. Apple's iCloud sync and Private Cloud Compute may process data on Apple's infrastructure outside your home country; that is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and data-transfer safeguards, not OneLocal's.

5.Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. In Packed's case, these are all already fully in your own hands: delete a trip, an item or the app itself, and that data is gone — there is no separate OneLocal-held copy to action a request against. You can still email support.packed@onelocal.dev with any privacy question, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

6.Children's privacy

Packed is not directed at children and knowingly collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

7.Data breaches

Since Packed holds no personal data outside your own device and your own iCloud account, there is no OneLocal-held dataset that could be breached. If that ever changes, this section will be updated to describe how you'd be notified.

8.Changes to this policy

If what the app does ever changes, this page will be updated to match before that change ships, with the date below revised accordingly.
