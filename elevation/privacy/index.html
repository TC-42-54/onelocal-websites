Privacy Policy

For the Elevation iOS and iPadOS app, published by OneLocal. This policy is written to meet GDPR's Article 13/14 disclosure requirements.

Elevation has no account, no sign-in, and no server of its own. Everything you draw, scan or write stays on your device unless you personally choose to export or share it.

1.Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is OneLocal (Thomas Chézières), an individual developer based in France. For anything in this policy: support.elevation@onelocal.dev. No separate Data Protection Officer is appointed — OneLocal does not carry out large-scale or systematic processing of the kind that requires one, and in practice processes none of your personal data at all (see below).

None of this is ever transmitted to OneLocal. There is no backend server, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting or advertising SDK in Elevation — OneLocal has no technical means of seeing your plans, your photos, or how you use the app.

3.Data Elevation does not collect

No account, no email address, no advertising identifier, no location, no contacts, no precise usage analytics. Elevation does not use cookies or any similar tracking technology, and does not perform any automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22).

4.International data transfers

Because nothing leaves your device, OneLocal does not transfer any of your data internationally. Apple's iOS share sheet (used only for exports you explicitly trigger) is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and data-transfer safeguards, not OneLocal's.

5.Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. In Elevation's case, these are all already fully in your own hands: delete a project, a photo or the app itself, and that data is gone — there is no separate copy held by OneLocal to action a request against. You can still email support.elevation@onelocal.dev with any privacy question, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

6.Children's privacy

Elevation is not directed at children and knowingly collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

7.Data breaches

Since Elevation holds no personal data outside your own device, there is no OneLocal-held dataset that could be breached. If that ever changes, this section will be updated to describe how you'd be notified.

8.Changes to this policy

If what the app does ever changes, this page will be updated to match before that change ships, with the date below revised accordingly.
