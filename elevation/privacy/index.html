OneLocal Elevation

Privacy Policy

For the Elevation iOS and iPadOS app, published by OneLocal.

Elevation has no account, no sign-in, and no server of its own. Everything you draw, scan or write stays on your device unless you personally choose to export or share it. This page explains exactly what the app touches and why.

What the app uses, and why

  • Your plans, photos and checklist. Every floor plan, room, pipe, task and attached photo is stored locally in the app's own on-device database. Nothing is uploaded automatically, and Elevation does not sync to iCloud or any server.
  • Camera. Used to scan a room with LiDAR to draw that floor's walls, and to photograph pipes, meters or finished work to attach to your plan and checklist. Photos never leave your device.
  • Photo library (optional). You can attach an existing photo from your library to a task instead of taking a new one. The app only sees the photo you pick, through Apple's standard picker.
  • Exports you ask for. The PDF, SketchUp and 3D-scan export buttons generate a file on your device and hand it to iOS's own share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose a destination yourself.
  • Language setting. Your English/French choice is saved locally with no connection to any account.

What OneLocal can see

Nothing. Elevation has no backend, no analytics, and no crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. There is no way for us to see your plans, your photos, or how you use the app.

Third parties

Elevation does not use any third-party services, trackers or ad networks.

Children's privacy

Elevation is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Your choices

Everything lives in the app's own storage, so deleting a project, or deleting the app, deletes that data. There is no server copy to separately request or delete.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does ever changes, this page will be updated to match before that change ships.
