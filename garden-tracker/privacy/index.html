OneLocal Garden Tracker

Privacy Policy

For the Garden Tracker iPhone app, published by OneLocal. This policy is written to meet GDPR's Article 13/14 disclosure requirements.

Garden Tracker has no account and no server of its own. Your garden is stored on your device and, if you turn sync on, kept private between your own devices through your own iCloud account.

1.Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is OneLocal (Thomas Chézières), an individual developer based in France. For anything in this policy: support.gardentracker@onelocal.dev. No separate Data Protection Officer is appointed — OneLocal does not carry out large-scale or systematic processing of the kind that requires one, and in practice processes none of your personal data on any server it operates (see below).

None of this is ever transmitted to OneLocal. There is no backend server, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting or advertising SDK in Garden Tracker. WeatherKit and Private Cloud Compute are Apple's own first-party services, governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy, not a third party OneLocal integrates separately.

3.Data Garden Tracker does not collect

No account, no email address, no advertising identifier, no contacts, no precise usage analytics. Garden Tracker does not use cookies or any similar tracking technology, and does not perform any automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22) — plant identification only ever suggests a name for you to confirm.

4.International data transfers

OneLocal itself does not transfer any of your data internationally, since it operates no server. Apple's iCloud sync, WeatherKit, and Private Cloud Compute may process data on Apple's infrastructure outside your home country; that is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and data-transfer safeguards, not OneLocal's.

5.Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. In Garden Tracker's case, these are all already fully in your own hands: delete a planting, turn off location access, or delete the app itself, and that data is gone — there is no separate OneLocal-held copy to action a request against. You can still email support.gardentracker@onelocal.dev with any privacy question, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

6.Children's privacy

Garden Tracker is not directed at children and knowingly collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

7.Data breaches

Since Garden Tracker holds no personal data outside your own device and your own iCloud account, there is no OneLocal-held dataset that could be breached. If that ever changes, this section will be updated to describe how you'd be notified.

8.Changes to this policy

If what the app does ever changes, this page will be updated to match before that change ships, with the date below revised accordingly.
