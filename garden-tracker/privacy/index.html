OneLocal Garden Tracker

Privacy Policy

For the Garden Tracker iPhone app, published by OneLocal.

Garden Tracker has no account and no server of its own. Your garden is yours — stored on your device and, if you turn iCloud sync on, kept private between your own devices through your own iCloud account. This page explains exactly what the app touches and why.

What the app uses, and why

  • Your beds, plantings and journal. Stored locally in the app's own on-device database. If you enable sync in Settings, the same data is kept across your devices privately through your own iCloud account — OneLocal has no access to it either way.
  • Camera. Takes photos of your plants and seed packets. Photos stay on your device.
  • Location (when in use). Used once to find your département and altitude, so planting dates and reminders fit your local climate. It is also sent, by Apple's own WeatherKit framework, to Apple to retrieve your local weather forecast — see "Third parties" below. OneLocal never receives your location directly.
  • On-device intelligence. Identifying a plant from a photo and reading a seed packet normally run entirely on your iPhone. For some requests, iOS may route part of the processing through Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designed to be privacy-preserving and independently verifiable — see Apple's own Privacy Policy for how that works. OneLocal has no access to this either way.
  • Widget. Shows your next tasks using the same on-device data as the app. It collects nothing separately.

What OneLocal can see

Nothing. Garden Tracker has no backend, no analytics, and no crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. There is no way for us to see your garden, your photos, or how you use the app.

Third parties

Garden Tracker does not use any third-party trackers or ad networks. Weather forecasts are provided by Apple's WeatherKit, and on-device intelligence may use Apple's Private Cloud Compute — both are Apple's own first-party services, governed by Apple's own privacy policy, not a third party OneLocal integrates separately.

Children's privacy

Garden Tracker is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Your choices

Everything lives in the app's own storage or your own iCloud account, so deleting a planting, turning off sync, or deleting the app removes that data. There is no separate OneLocal copy to request or delete. Location access can be turned off in iOS Settings at any time; the app still works, just without climate-matched dates.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does ever changes, this page will be updated to match before that change ships.
